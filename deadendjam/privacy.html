What data is processed?

  • Game data (levels, stars, gold, lives, preferences) stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • No account is created. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Ad data: when an ad is shown, the ad provider Google AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, device details and ad interactions.

Why, and on what legal basis?

Data needed to provide the game is processed to perform our contract with you and for our legitimate interests. Personalized ads are shown only with your explicit consent. If you don't consent, you can still play the whole game.

Who receives it?

Ad data is shared with Google to serve ads and may be processed abroad.

How is it collected?

Automatically, through the app and its ad software.

Retention and deletion

Game data is deleted with “Delete my data” in Settings or when you uninstall the app. Google's retention terms apply to ad data.

Withdrawing consent

You can turn off “Personalized ads” in Settings at any time.

Your rights (Turkish KVKK Art. 11 and, where it applies, GDPR)

  • Learn whether your data is processed and request information
  • Learn why it is processed and whether it is used for that purpose
  • Learn which third parties receive it
  • Ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and for recipients to be told
  • Object to a result against you that comes from automated analysis
  • Claim compensation for harm caused by unlawful processing
